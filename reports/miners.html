How the value is calculated

  1. Owner earnings = adjusted net income + D&A − sustaining capex, × periods a year.
  2. Re-price to today's metals: ounces / pounds sold × (spot − realised price) × (1 − royalty) × (1 − tax).
  3. Value = owner earnings × 12 (cautious) and × 15 (fair), plus net cash per share.
  4. Rule: BUY needs a 20% margin of safety at 12x and AISC ≤ 60% of spot. 25x and above is the sell zone.
Worked example: (183 + 119 − 133) × 4 ÷ 1,317.2m shares = $0.51 a share; re-priced to today's metals −0.15 → $0.36. × 12 − $0.20 net debt = $4.18 (cautious); × 15 = $5.27 (fair). Price $4.96.
