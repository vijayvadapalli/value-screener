How the value is calculated

  1. Earnings per share: cyclical, but the 5-year average is not used (FY22–FY25 are the old combined Tata Motors incl. commercial vehicles, and FY26 holds the ₹76,248 cr demerger gain): 12-month EPS -6.69.
  2. Value = 5 years of earnings growing at g, then sold at 15x, discounted at 12% (no-growth multiple 12.1x), plus net cash.
  3. Growth needed = the yearly growth that makes the value equal the price.
  4. Rule: green if it needs ≤ 5% a year, blue 5–25%, red ≥ 25%. Cyclicals and cash conversion below 70% are capped at blue.

Assumptions and data notes

  • Shares derived from Screener's market value ÷ price
  • Reported 12-month EPS −6.69 (Dec 25 −9.47, Mar 26 15.70, Jun 26 2.10, Sep 26 −15.02): no positive earnings, so not valued (red).
  • Derived, for scale only: adding back the Sep 2026 quarter's ₹6,738 cr exceptional charge (pre-tax, ≈ ₹18.3/share) gives 12-month EPS ≈ ₹11.6 and a no-growth value ≈ ₹49 (11.6 × 12.1 − 91) against the ₹287 price.
  • The price is a bet on a JLR margin recovery (company target 6–8%, FY27 guidance 2–3%) and on the Indian car business. Both are forecasts and are not in the value.
  • Watch: whether "exceptional" charges keep recurring (cyberattack, restructuring, write-downs), and free cash flow / net debt, not just profit.
