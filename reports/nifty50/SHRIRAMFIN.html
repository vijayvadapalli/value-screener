How the value is calculated

  1. Earnings per share: financial: average of 12-month EPS 59.84 and adjusted book 443.74 (book 474.00 − bad loans not provided for 30.26) × 5-year ROE 16.0% = 71.00.
  2. Value = 5 years of earnings growing at g, then sold at 15x, discounted at 12% (no-growth multiple 12.1x), with no net-cash adjustment for a financial.
  3. Growth needed = the yearly growth that makes the value equal the price.
  4. Rule: green if it needs ≤ 5% a year, blue 5–25%, red ≥ 25%. Cyclicals and cash conversion below 70% are capped at blue.
  5. Bad loans: book value is cut by the bad loans not yet provided for (net NPA × loans). Net NPA above 2%, gross NPA above 5%, provision coverage below 60% or gross NPA rising by more than half a point in a year caps the signal at blue. Capital ratios are not checked – look them up.
Worked example: EPS ₹65.42 (financial: average of 12-month EPS 59.84 and adjusted book 443.74 (book 474.00 − bad loans not provided for 30.26) × 5-year ROE 16.0% = 71.00) × 12.1 (no-growth multiple at 12%) + ₹0.00 net cash = ₹792.64.
