How the value is calculated

  1. Earnings per share: 12-month EPS 35.56.
  2. Value = 5 years of earnings growing at g, then sold at 15x, discounted at 12% (no-growth multiple 12.1x), plus net cash.
  3. Growth needed = the yearly growth that makes the value equal the price.
  4. Rule: green if it needs ≤ 5% a year, blue 5–25%, red ≥ 25%. Cyclicals and cash conversion below 70% are capped at blue.
