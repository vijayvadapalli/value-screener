How it is built. Every holding needs a margin of safety on its own model (producers at least 20% below the 12x value or tier A at or below 15x; growth stocks needing at most 5–8% a year), quality (no stale or flagged data, AISC ≤ 60% of spot, debt ≤ 3× EBITDA, no heavy dilution, cash conversion ≥ 70%, grade not D; no developers, explorers or tier-C juniors) and is scored 60% value, 25% quality, 15% momentum. Momentum decides how much to buy now: at or above 95% of the 200-day average with a positive 6-month return = full position, otherwise start a third and keep the rest in T-bills. Weights within a sleeve follow inverse volatility, at most 10% per name; a sleeve without enough qualifying names is filled with its ETF. Rebalance monthly, or when a holding's signal turns red.
